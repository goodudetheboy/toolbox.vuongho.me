Added `apps/study-assistant` — see its own CLAUDE.md, ADR 0001 and progress
log. Toolbox-wide changes:

  databases in `firebase.json`). Without the variable, CI behaves as before.
- `firebase.json`: `/study-assistant/**` rewrite + the new database's rules.
