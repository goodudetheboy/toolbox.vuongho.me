# How to use Churn Machine

_Last updated: October 16, 2026_

Churn Machine finds bank account bonuses, builds a plan for which accounts to open with your paycheck, and walks you through running that plan day by day. The deals come from Doctor of Credit's [Best Bank Account Bonuses](https://www.doctorofcredit.com/best-bank-account-bonuses/) list. They're refreshed every Monday morning and whenever the app is updated. Always read the full post before opening an account.

> Always read the Doctor of Credit post for a deal before opening the account. Anything marked "assumed" wasn't in the post.

## The three tabs

- **Deals**: every bonus, searchable and filterable.
- **Plan**: tell it your paycheck and it suggests strategies (which accounts to open, when, and how to split each paycheck).
- **My Bag**: the plans you saved, with what to do today and how much you've collected. Needs Google sign-in.

The round button at the top right (your photo and name once you sign in) opens a small menu with your state, this guide, and sign in or sign out. Your state hides deals you can't get where you live. It applies everywhere.

## Deals

### Finding deals

- **Search** by bank name.
- **Chips** for quick filters: No direct deposit, Online only, No hard pull, No parking cash.
- **Sort** with the menu next to "All deals": best value, biggest bonus, ending soonest, least effort, best return on parked cash, or Doctor of Credit's own order.
- **Filters** (right sidebar on a computer, the "Filters" chip on a phone): business accounts, minimum bonus, the most direct deposit you can route, the most cash you can park, your savings APY, ChexSystems-sensitive banks, and whether to show deals past their end date or ended.

Each row shows the bonus in the purple pill and the **net** value under it (bonus minus the interest you'd give up on parked cash and any fees you can't avoid).

### Leaving out banks

In the **Banks** box (sidebar, or the Filters sheet on a phone):

- **Exclude banks I already have**: hides every bank in your "Banks I have" list plus any account you've opened through the app. On by default. Needs sign-in.
- **Exclude banks**: pick any other banks to hide. This list is saved on this device.

The planner leaves out the same banks.

### Upfront vs later money

Some deals advertise more than you'll get for the sign-up requirements: part of the bonus only comes after you keep direct deposits going for 12 or 24 months, or it's paid a little each month for a year. Every deal on the list was checked by hand, and the app only counts the **upfront** part, the money you get once the sign-up requirements are done. Values, sorting, filters and plans all use it.

- A deal's page shows "Listed as $X" when the headline differs, what earns the upfront bonus, and **Later, not counted** with any long-haul money.
- In the list, a deal with long-haul money shows "+$X later, not counted".
- A deal that only pays over a year or more counts as $0 and isn't planned.
- New deals, and deals whose post was rewritten since the check, use the automatic read of the post; their page says so.

### A deal's details

Click a deal to open it. You'll see the bonus (the **upfront** part, see below), the net value and grade, **Key statistics** (direct deposit, cash to park, transactions, how long to keep it open, credit pull, when the bonus posts), **Is it worth it?** (the math behind net), the offer's terms, every detail from the post, and Doctor of Credit's take.

- **Open full page** gives the deal its own page and link, with the at-a-glance lines from the list and an "Open at" card.
- **Add to custom plan** puts the deal in your custom plan (no sign-in needed). Once it's in, **Build plan** takes you to it and **Remove** takes it out. You mark an account as opened later, from the plan's checklist when you run it.

## Plan

### Build your plan

Plan walks you through it one screen at a time. **← back** links (and your browser's back button) go to the previous screen, and your answers are remembered.

**Step 1, Your paycheck:** just what's needed to get started.

- **Paycheck**: the take-home pay from one paycheck that you can route. Paychecks are on the 1st and 15th.
- **Start**: the day the plan begins (today unless you pick a later date). Nothing is opened or deposited before it, and the plan length counts from it.
- **Next payday**: filled in for you (the first 1st or 15th on or after the start).

Then **Next**.

**Step 2, How do you want to plan?** Choose **Suggest strategies** (we pick the deals and show strategies to compare) or **Build my own** (you pick the deals, see Custom plans below).

**Step 3, the plans.** Suggested plans are listed under **Plans for you**. Every setting is right there in **Your settings** (the panel on the right; on a phone, tap it to open), so you never have to go back to change something:

- Paycheck, Start, Next payday, and **Plan for** (3, 6, 9 or 12 months; 6 to start).
- **More options and filters**: the most accounts a plan can have, the two bank exclusions (same as on Deals), cash you can park, how many accounts one paycheck can be split across, how many days ahead of payday to open accounts, deals past their listed end date, the smallest bonus worth doing, and avoid hard pulls, online only, ChexSystems, business accounts. **Reset** puts them back.

### Custom plans

Choose **Build my own**, then add the deals you want on **Pick your deals** (type a bank's name, or open the list). Deals you added with **Add to custom plan** while browsing are already there. Each one you pick shows as a tile with its bonus and main requirements. **Build my plan** shows **Your plan**, with the same **Your settings** panel (your deals are in it too, to add or remove; the filters aren't, since they don't apply). The planner schedules only those, trying to fit all of them around your paychecks, and shows a Relaxed, Steady and Aggressive version. Each row says how many of your picks fit.

Since you chose the deals, the filters (minimum bonus, hard pulls, online only, ChexSystems, business, bank exclusions) don't apply. Your state, cash to park, paycheck splits, lead days and "Include deals past their listed end date" still do. If a pick doesn't fit, the strategy says why: for example it isn't offered in your state, it needs more cash parked than you set, its end date is before the plan starts, or it doesn't fit around the others (try a longer plan, a faster pace or more paycheck splits).

Custom plans can be shared, saved and tracked like any other. In **Edit settings** you can add or remove deals.

### Strategies

Each strategy is a **goal** × **pace**:

- Goals: **Most money** (highest net first), **Big ones only** ($300+ net), **Least effort** (simple requirements, no branch visits or hard pulls), **Money soonest** (fastest payout first).
- Paces: **Relaxed**, **Steady**, **Aggressive** (up to 1, 2 or 3 new accounts a month).
- Custom plans use the same paces with your own deals.

Rows show the number of accounts, cash parked (if any), a small chart, and the total net in the pill. Sort them with the menu above the list.

### A strategy's details

Click a strategy to open it:

- The big number and chart show bonuses adding up over time. Move your mouse or finger along the chart (or use the arrow keys) to see each payout.
- **Accounts to open** (shown first): each account with what it needs and its bonus. Click a bank to see its deal.
- **Schedule**: date by date, which accounts to open and how much of that paycheck goes to which bank. Paydays where everything stays in your main account are left out. Cash to park, promo codes and payout dates are on each deal's page, and in the plan's checklist once you save it.
- **Share link** copies a link to this exact strategy (on a phone it opens the share menu). Anyone can open it, signed in or not. It includes the plan's settings and the banks it leaves out. It's rebuilt from that day's deals, so it can differ if the deals changed; a note says so.
- If someone else's link has different settings from yours, **Use these settings in my plan** copies them into your form.
- **Use this plan** saves it to My Bag and takes you to its tracker.

## My Bag

### Home

- **Collected so far** across all plans, and how much is on the way.
- **To do today**: today's and overdue tasks from every active plan. Tap the circle to mark one done, or the rest of the row to open its plan.
- **Active plans**: each with a progress bar, the day of the plan (or "Starts Dec 1" if it starts later), tasks today and anything overdue.
- **Finished and archived** plans, folded away.
- **Banks I have**: the banks you have or had an account with. Pick from the list, or type any name and choose "Add". Accounts you've opened through the app count automatically. This list is saved to your Google account.
- **Accounts outside a plan**: accounts you added by hand (**Add an account by hand**), and any you tracked from a deal before that button became Add to custom plan.

### Running a plan

Open a plan to see:

- Today's date and **day X of Y**.
- **Collected** out of the planned total, and what's on the way.
- **Don't forget**: for accounts you've marked opened, the requirements besides deposits and parked cash (debit card purchases, eStatements, online banking, small minimum balances), so you don't miss them. Tap the circle when one's done. It goes away once the account's requirements are done.
- **Today**: the tasks due today. Tap the circle next to one when it's done (tap it again to undo). For a bonus, put the amount you actually received in the box first, then tap the circle.
- **Next two weeks**.
- **The whole plan**: every task month by month, with a "Today" line. Tap a filled circle to undo a task you marked by mistake.
- **Accounts in this plan**: click one to edit its dates, status, the bonus amount and notes.
- **Revisions**: a log of every revise or settings change.

Tasks you'll see: open the account, a paycheck deposit landing, moving cash in to park (and when you can move it out), requirements done, the bonus posting, and when it's safe to close (optional).

### Falling behind: check-in and Revise

If something you had to do is past due and not done, a **check-in** box lists it. Mark any you actually did. For the rest, press **Revise plan**:

- Accounts you already opened stay. Missed deposits move onto your next paychecks, before the bank's deadline. If one can't make it in time, it's flagged so you can check with the bank.
- Accounts you haven't opened yet are re-picked from today with the same goal and pace, skipping banks you have.
- You see a preview (planned total before and after, and every change) before anything is saved.

A bonus that's late shows under **Waiting on the bank** instead. Banks are often slow; mark it when it lands.

### Changing a plan's settings

**Edit settings** (in the gear menu next to the plan's name) lets you change the goal (or, for a custom plan, its deals), pace, paycheck, length, cash, splits, lead days, minimum bonus, options and banks to leave out. A live preview shows what changes. **Save and re-plan** applies it the same way as Revise: opened accounts stay, the rest is re-planned from today.

### Manage this plan

Tap the gear next to the plan's name: **Revise from today** (any time, even if you're not behind), **Edit settings**, **Archive**, and **Delete plan** (accounts you never opened go with it; opened ones stay in your records).

## Install it as an app

On a phone, use your browser's "Add to Home Screen" (Safari: Share, then Add to Home Screen). It opens full screen and works offline with the last deals it loaded. When there's a new version, a small "Update" bar appears.

On a phone, deals, plans and settings open as cards from the bottom. Swipe one down to close it (from the top of the card, or anywhere once you've scrolled back to the top).

## What's saved where

- **Your Google account** (synced): saved plans, accounts, task check-offs, and "Banks I have".
- **This device only**: your state, filters, sort, plan form, the "Exclude banks I already have" switch and the extra "Exclude banks" list.

## Words you'll see

- **DD**: direct deposit, usually from your paycheck. Most bonuses need a minimum amount within a window of days.
- **Upfront**: what a deal pays once its sign-up requirements are done. Money that needs a year or more of keeping deposits or balances going is shown as "later" and never counted.
- **Net**: the upfront bonus minus interest you'd give up on parked cash (at your savings APY) and fees you can't avoid.
- **Park cash**: money you keep in the account for a set time to earn the bonus.
- **Keep open**: how long to keep the account open so the bank doesn't take the bonus back.
- **Hard pull**: a credit check that shows on your credit report. A soft pull doesn't.
- **ChexSystems**: a banking history report some banks check; opening many accounts can get you declined at sensitive banks.
- **Assumed**: a value the post didn't give, so the app used a typical one. Check the post.
