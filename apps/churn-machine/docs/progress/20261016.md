## 03:10 — Custom plans and a start date

At the user's request (ADR 0010):
- **Custom plans**: Plan has a Suggested / Custom switch. In Custom you pick deals ("Deals you want", a searchable picker labelled "Bank · $bonus") and the planner schedules only those at each pace, ignoring the preference filters. Rows say "N of M deals fit". The strategy modal lists every pick that didn't fit and why (state, cash to park, end date before start, ended, terms unreadable, or "doesn't fit around the others"). If none fit, the list shows the reasons directly. Custom plans share (`only=`), save, track, revise, and in Edit settings the goal picker becomes a deal picker.
- **Start date**: a "Start" row in the Plan form (today by default). The planner plans from `max(today, startOn)`: paydays roll to it, nothing opens before it, the length counts from it. It's in the permalink (`start=`) and the modal's summary, saved with the plan (so `endsOn` counts from it), and My plans / the tracker say "Starts <date>" until then.
- Planner: `custom` objective, `SUGGESTED` list for the suggested goals, `pickBlocker` reasons, `Strategy.missed`, `planStartOn`. `DealPicker` in `bits.tsx` wraps `BankPicker` (which gained a `removeTitle`).

Verified: 32 unit tests (2 new: custom picks ignore filters and report a pick outside your state; a later start opens nothing and pays nothing before it, and a past start means today). Production build in headless Chromium at 1280 and 390 px: start Dec 1 → next payday Dec 1, the modal says "Starts Dec 1", the link carries `start=`; Custom with 6 picks → "1 of 6 deals fit" with correct reasons; the copied link rebuilds the same $300 plan in a fresh page; no console errors. Not exercised in a browser: saving a custom/future plan and its tracker (needs the emulators); covered by types and the shared code path.

Open:
- Picks are whole deals at their top tier, so combo deals (e.g. Chase $900 checking + savings) say "needs $15,000 parked" instead of being planned for the checking-only part. Would need tier-level parsing.
- Edit settings doesn't offer changing the start date yet.
- An "Add to custom plan" button on a deal's modal would be a natural next step.
