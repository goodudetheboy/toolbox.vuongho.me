# 2026-11-15

## 14:00 — QR code to share the app

- New QR button left of the bookmark on the setup (pick your stops) screen.
  It opens a SHARE sheet (`src/screens/QrSheet.tsx`) with a QR code for
  `toolbox.vuongho.me/njtransit-bus/` so a friend can scan it off the phone.
  Drawn as an SVG with `qrcode-generator` (new dependency, no deps of its
  own), always black on white so scanners read it on either color scheme.
- It shares the app only, not your stops: the app has no way to open a trip
  from a link yet.
- Checked on the production build in headless Chromium: button sits left of
  the bookmark, sheet opens, and the screenshot's QR decodes (jsQR) to the
  app's link. The dev container has no Chromium system libraries or fonts and
  no root; `apt-get download` + `dpkg -x` into a scratch folder, then
  `LD_LIBRARY_PATH` (and `FONTCONFIG_FILE` for fonts-dejavu-core) works.
