## 13:10 — Countdown matches NJ Transit's app

A friend saw 9 min on our board next to 12 min in NJ Transit's app (12:21, Palisade Ave at 16th St → PABT). Logged raw `getBusDV` every 30 s across the 2:05 PM 123 and compared against the user's screenshots of the official app: the official app shows `departurestatus` ("in 15 mins") as given, and its "Bus #…" is `vehicle_id` — the same feed we read. We were using `departuretime` instead, a whole-minute clock that lags the countdown (read 02:05 while the countdown put the late bus at 2:07), and then rounding down again, so we read 1–3 min low. Now: tracked bus = fetch time + N min (`api/njt.js`), minutes rounded to nearest (`format.ts`), and live buses also show the clock time they leave ("LEAVES 2:07 PM" under the big number, the time under each tile's minutes). ADR 0001 amended. Checked against the local API with live data (4 min vs "in 4 mins"); not looked at in a browser, because the headless browser here is missing a system library (libglib) and there's no root to install it.

Open: the exact 12:21 case (3 min off) can't be replayed; the lagging clock plus round-down explains most of it, but a stale late-trip entry isn't ruled out.
