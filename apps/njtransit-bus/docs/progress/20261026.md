# 2026-10-26

## 08:15 — Settings sheet: how full + bus number

- What changed: a gear button sits right of the nerd button at the end of the
  list and opens a settings sheet (same look as the stats sheet) with two
  switches, both off by default and kept in `localStorage`
  (`njtransit-bus:settings`): **HOW FULL** (a row of three people, filled
  1/2/3 for EMPTY / HALF_EMPTY / FULL) and **BUS NUMBER** (`BUS #6386`).
  Two sample tiles (FULL and HALF_EMPTY) are stuck to the bottom of the sheet
  and change as the switches flip. When on, both also show on the hero under
  the route. `Tile` moved out of `Board.tsx` into `Tile.tsx` so the sheet can
  reuse it.
- API: `/departures` now sends `vehicle` on every bus (BUSDV2's vehicle id,
  else the GTFS-realtime one), and `load` drops BUSDV2's "no data" (only
  `EMPTY`/`HALF_EMPTY`/`FULL`-style values get through). Checked against the
  live feed: at Port Authority BUSDV2 actually answers `HALF_EMPTY`, `FULL`
  and `no data`; `EMPTY` is assumed from the naming, not seen yet.
- Decisions: no ADR, a small display option.
- TODOs: the watch app doesn't use either field.

## 08:35 — Fix: every bus listed twice (today's run + tomorrow's)

- From a stats-sheet paste at Boulevard East at 62nd St → Port Authority:
  every trip came back twice, `scheduled` exactly 24 h apart, both carrying
  today's GPS prediction. GTFS trip ids repeat every service day and the
  timetable window reaches 24 h ahead, so tomorrow's run of a trip matched
  today's BUSDV2/trip-updates/GPS entries by id. The doubles filled the
  12-bus cap and pushed out real buses (the 166 the NJT app showed).
- Fix (`api/index.js`): the feeds only apply to each trip id's earliest run
  in the window. Checked live at the same stop: one row per trip, the 166
  (#33, BUSDV2) is back.
- Noticed, not acted on: at rush hour most 128s there are GPS-only and 15–30
  min late; the NJT app lists far fewer buses than we do.

## 08:45 — How full: read it from the GPS feed too

- "How full" showed on almost nothing: only BUSDV2 sends `passload`, and at
  rush hour most buses on the board are GPS-only. GTFS-realtime vehicle
  positions carry `occupancy_status` (field 9) for 1579 of 1612 buses; NJ
  Transit only uses 2 (FEW_SEATS_AVAILABLE) and 5 (FULL), and on 75 buses
  both feeds covered they matched HALF_EMPTY/FULL every time.
- `parseVehicles` now returns `load` (0–1 EMPTY, 2 HALF_EMPTY, 3–5 FULL);
  `/departures` uses BUSDV2's load, else the GPS one. ADR 0004 got an addendum
  (it said GPS buses have no load).
- In practice it's two people (half full) or three (full); one person never
  shows since NJ Transit never sends EMPTY.
